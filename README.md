## The Features That Actually Matter

Let me walk you through what this thing can do.

### Back in Stock Notifications

This is the bread and butter. When products run out, customers can sign up to get notified. The app automatically sends them an email (or SMS, or push notification, or WhatsApp—your choice) when inventory's replenished.

The smart part? You can set minimum inventory thresholds. So if you only have 2 units left, you might not want to blast notifications to 200 people. The system handles that.

### Preorder Management

Here's where things get clever. Out of stock doesn't have to mean "come back later." With preorders enabled, customers can buy now and receive later. You can offer discounts to sweeten the deal, accept partial payments, and keep the cash flowing even when shelves are empty.

The app processes the orders, sends confirmation emails, and handles fulfillment triggers when inventory arrives. It's like having a product launch system built into your regular store.

### Low Stock Alerts

Ever create urgency by showing "Only 3 left!"? That's what the low stock widget does. It displays inventory counts to nudge fence-sitters into action. FOMO works, whether we like it or not.

### Wishlist Functionality

Customers can save products for later, even without logging in (guest wishlists). This reduces cart abandonment and gives you valuable data about what people want. Plus, you can send reminder emails to wishlist users during sales.

### AI-Powered Inventory Insights

The newest addition is Notify Me AI. It analyzes millions of data points from requests, restocks, and orders to tell you what to restock, when to launch preorders, and which products might sell faster than expected.

Think of it as having a business analyst who never sleeps and only cares about your inventory decisions.
